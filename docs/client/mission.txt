Mission
​At VERSA, we believe hydration should be both premium and sustainable. Founded by a team passionate about innovation and the planet, we’ve redefined what it means to drink responsibly. VERSA provides custom-branded canned water and reusable aluminum bottles for hotels, food franchises, events, and venues worldwide. Each product is designed to replace single-use plastic and deliver a cleaner, smarter, and more stylish drinking experience.
​We manage every stage—from design and production to delivery, storage, and recycling—so our partners can focus on what they do best. Through our closed-loop ReCan programme, every can is collected, recycled, and reborn, benefitting clients financially by transforming waste into value and turning the cost of waste management into a new revenue stream. VERSA makes sustainability seamless, empowering businesses to showcase their values and reduce plastic waste without compromising luxury, convenience, or brand identity
